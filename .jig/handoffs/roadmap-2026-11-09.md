## Built
<!-- what this delivers, in a few lines -->
An optional per-repo roadmap. `.jig/roadmap.md` holds phases (`## <phase>`), each with an
`Exit when:` checklist and a `Scopes:` list. Specs take scopes with a `Scope:` line
(many-to-many). A spec is complete when a non-partial handoff naming it has landed on trunk
(`jig handoff --partial` for a chunk). Scope states (open, taken, done) and phase states
(done, current, next, empty) are derived from that. `jig roadmap` prints them and flags a
stale or missing view. `jig roadmap --write` and `jig merge` (in the merge commit) generate
the root `ROADMAP.md`: a phase line, then per phase an Open/Taken/Done Markdown table.
Staging gains `kind: roadmap`, resolved by its mark in a trunk commit to `.jig/roadmap.md`.
R5 covers both files. The hook refuses unknown or dropped scopes and malformed roadmaps.
Survey counts `ROADMAP.md` as a home. A repo with no `.jig/roadmap.md` is untouched.
