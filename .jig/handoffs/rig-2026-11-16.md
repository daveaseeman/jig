## Built
`jig rig` (jig.py `cmd_rig` and its helpers): the repo's picture as text and `--json`. It lists ready specs, staged files and the roadmap's next step, with what each waits on, its group, how it `opens` (now, after or answer), the overlapping pairs and dependency cycles, and the open jobs with `jig next`'s step. The order is fixed, so the same state prints the same bytes. The skill's open/ready judgments are stored in `.git/jig/rig.json`, keyed by item content and the board (`--store-put`, `--store-get`, `--fresh`). Specs carrying `Bug-for:` are no longer waiting specs.
`/jig:rig` (new skill): judges unjudged items, draws the picture, puts one batch of decisions through `/jig:decision-cards`, and acts on the answers. `auto` writes specs and opens the specs that can start, and leaves the owner's questions open. No aliases: "what's next" triggers nothing, and `jig next` is unchanged.
Also built here: bug-rig-design-in-review's §4 items 1-3 (`.jig/specs/bug-rig-design-in-review.md`, Bug-for: rig).
