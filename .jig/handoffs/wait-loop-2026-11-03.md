## Review
Round 1: a fresh claude-opus-5-5 subagent, with effort set to xhigh. It reported its effort as unknown,
and the review is recorded that way. It found 3 blocking, 3 should-fix and 2 optional.
- S1, root cause corrected. On macOS, `pgrep` excludes its own process and all its ancestors, so the loop
  never matched itself. Pid 28194 waited on matches that never cleared: other sessions' `bash tests/smoke.sh`
  runs, and "isolation:" in a log deleted with its worktree. Fixed: the spec's Why 1 and Givens are rewritten,
  the verify rule is "never wait on a match" (a process pattern or a log line), and the replay checks for
  any leftover wait loop.
- B1: no `Source:` line held the symptom. Fixed: bug step 5, the spec template, review item 1 and `/jig:fix`.
- B2: the `kill -0 $pid` fallback hit the foreground limit when run in one command, and saw an empty `$pid`
  when split across commands. Fixed: `& echo $!`, then a bounded poll of the literal pid in each later command.
- B3: the evidence needed keeping. Kept: the replay's prompt and result, and the smoke output, in the notes folder.
- S2: added the verify pointer to the hubnode's Release and survey bullet.
- S3: the hook alternative's reason is now `[seen]` (S1's evidence).
- O1: the wait is capped at about 2× the usual run, then you read the output and report it.
- O2 (a reused pid): deferred, with a row in `.jig/ledgers/DEFERRED.md`, ranked optional.
All taken items are fixed in 894c09a.
